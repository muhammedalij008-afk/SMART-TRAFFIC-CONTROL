Control logic

Five rules, repeated every cycle

The adaptive controller reads the queue on each road and re-allocates green time after every clearance interval. It needs no schedule.

The fixed timer

Gives each road 25 s of green in order A, B, C, D. It never looks at the queue, so an empty road still gets its full turn.

  1. Clear the junction

    When a green ends, every signal holds red for 4 s so cars already inside can leave.

  2. Score each road

    Every other road gets a score of queue length plus 0.2 × seconds since its last green. The second term keeps small roads from starving.

    Road A: 10 cars, 12 s since green → 12.4
    Road D: 4 cars, 40 s since green → 12.0
    A goes first. D is close behind.

  3. Size the green

    The winner gets 2 s per queued car plus 4 s, held between 8 s and 45 s. Ten waiting cars earn a 24 s green.

  4. End early when empty

    After 5 s of green, if the queue has drained, the green ends and the next road is scored.

  5. Give way to an ambulance

    When an ambulance is detected, conflicting greens end after at least 5 s, the junction clears, and the ambulance's road gets green. The delay is shown in the panel.

Architecture

Decide at the intersection, learn in the cloud

On the road

Sensing

  • Queue length on each of the four approaches
  • Time since each road last had green
  • Emergency vehicle detection

At the intersection

Edge controller

  • Runs the five rules above on its own
  • Switches signal heads after every clearance interval
  • Keeps working if the network drops
Planned

In the cloud

Learning

  • Learns demand patterns by hour and weekday
  • Pushes tuned parameters to each edge controller
  • Compares junctions across a corridor

Queue counts flow left to right. Tuned parameters flow back to the edge.

Arrival rates0 to 0.40 veh/sRandom, set per road with the sliders.

A fixed cycle takes 4 × (25 + 4) = 116 s, so each road gets about 12 cars through per cycle. That is roughly 0.1 cars per second. Any road busier than that builds a queue under the fixed timer, which is what the default traffic does on roads A and D.
